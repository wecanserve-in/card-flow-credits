FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still stuck? Message us on WhatsApp or email.

How many cards can I scan at once?

As many as you like. Select them in the queue and the app processes them in batches.

How accurate is it?

Very accurate on clear, well-lit cards. You can review and fix every field before saving.

What if a scan fails?

You don't lose credits. Failed scans are refunded automatically.

Do credits expire?

No. Buy a pack today and use it next year if you like.

Can I export to Excel?

Yes. Export everything, or one folder, as .xlsx and share it anywhere: WhatsApp, email, Drive and so on.

Is my data private?

Yes. Only you can access your contacts. We don't sell data or use your cards for ads. Card images are processed to read the text and are not stored on our servers afterwards. Details are in our privacy policy.

How much does it cost?

You start with 5 free scans. After that, prepaid packs start at ₹99 for 50 cards (+18% GST). 1 credit = 1 card, with no subscription. See pricing.

How do I pay?

Inside the app, through Razorpay: UPI, debit and credit cards, netbanking and wallets. We never see your full card or UPI details.

Is there an iPhone app?

Android first; iOS is coming.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile tab → tap your profile card → Delete Account. Or request it on our account deletion page.
