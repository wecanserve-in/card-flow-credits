Account

Delete your ScanMyCard account

You can delete your account and data in the app, or ask us to do it here without installing the app.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open ScanMyCard.
  2. Tap Profile in the bottom bar.
  3. Tap your profile card at the top.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your account, saved contacts, folders, notifications, profile photo and purchase records. It cannot be undone.

Option 2: without the app

Fill in this form. It opens a pre-filled email to wecanserve23@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion request". Send it from the email address you use for ScanMyCard if you can, so we can verify the account.

Email app didn't open? Email us directly with your name and account email.

We'll delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in
  • All saved contacts
  • Folders
  • Events
  • Notifications
  • Profile photo

What may be kept

  • Payment records that our payment processor (Razorpay) and Indian tax law require us to keep, held only as long as legally required.

Unused credits are lost when the account is deleted. Card images are never stored after scanning, so there are none to delete. More in our privacy policy.
