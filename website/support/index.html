Quick answers

A scan failed. Did I lose a credit?

No. Failed scans are refunded to your balance automatically.

I paid but don't see my credits.

Credits are usually added within a few minutes. If not, message us within 7 days with your Razorpay payment ID. See the refund policy.

The details came out wrong.

Retake the photo in good light with the card filling most of the frame, and fix any field on the review screen before saving.

How do I delete my account?

Profile tab → tap your profile card → Delete Account, or use our deletion page.
