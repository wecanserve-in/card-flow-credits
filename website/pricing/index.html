Payments

Pay the way you already do.

You buy packs inside the app. Payments are handled by Razorpay, so you can use:

  • UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and other UPI apps)
  • Debit and credit cards
  • Netbanking
  • Wallets

We never see your full card number or UPI details. See our refund & cancellation policy.

Do credits expire?

No. Unused credits stay in your account until you use them.

What counts as one credit?

One business card scanned = one credit. If a scan fails, the credit goes back to your balance automatically.

Can I buy more than one pack?

Yes. Buy a pack whenever you need more cards.

Is there a subscription?

No. You only pay when you buy a pack. Nothing renews automatically.
