Who we are

ScanMyCard is an Android app, made by WeCanServe, that turns photos of paper business cards into digital contacts. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean WeCanServe, and "you" means anyone who uses the ScanMyCard app or this website.

This policy explains what information we collect, why, who helps us process it, and the choices you have. If you have any question about it, email wecanserve23@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google profile picture from Google. If you sign up with email, we store your email address and a password (handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see your password in plain text).
  • Profile photo (optional): if you upload one.
  • Business-card images: the photos you take or pick from your gallery for scanning.
  • Contact details from those cards: name, company, designation, phone, email, website, country and address, plus the folders you organise them into.

Information created when you use the app

  • Purchase history: which credit packs you bought, the amount, the date, your credit balance and the payment reference from our payment processor. We do not receive or store your full card number, CVV or UPI PIN.
  • Push-notification token: a device token that lets us send you notifications about scan results, low credits and payments.
  • Technical logs: like most online services, our servers and providers keep short-lived logs (such as IP address, time of request and error messages) to keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not read your phone's contact list or track your location. The app uses the camera only when you choose to scan a card, and your photo gallery only when you choose to pick a photo.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To read the text on the business cards you scan and show you the extracted details to review.
  • To save your contacts and folders to your account and sync them across your devices.
  • To create Excel exports when you ask for them.
  • To process credit purchases, keep your credit balance accurate and return credits when a scan fails.
  • To send notifications about scan results, low credits and payments.
  • To answer your support requests and keep records the law requires.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles.

How card images are processed

When you scan a card, the image is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and to Google's Gemini AI (through Google Cloud / Vertex AI) only to extract the text. The extracted details are sent back to your phone for you to review.

  • Card images are not stored on our servers after processing.
  • Card images are not used for advertising.
  • Only the contact details you choose to save are kept in your account.

Where data is stored

  • Google Firebase stores your account (Firebase Authentication) and your contacts, folders, notifications, credit balance and purchase records (Cloud Firestore).
  • Cloudflare R2 stores profile photos.

Service providers

We use a small number of trusted companies to run ScanMyCard. They process data on our behalf and only for the purposes below.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal data or your contacts, and we do not share them with advertisers. We share data only with the service providers listed above, when you choose to share an export yourself, or when the law requires it (for example, a valid order from a court or government authority).

Security

  • All data between the app, our server and our providers travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Database rules make each user's contacts, folders and records accessible only to that signed-in user.
  • Payments are handled by Razorpay, a PCI-DSS compliant payment processor.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you if a breach affects you, as the law requires.

Data retention

  • Card images: not stored after processing.
  • Account, contacts, folders, notifications and profile photo: kept while your account is active, and deleted when you delete your account.
  • Payment records: kept as long as Razorpay and Indian tax and accounting law require, and then deleted.
  • Technical logs: kept for a short period and then deleted automatically by our providers.

Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • Access your contacts at any time in the app, and export them to Excel.
  • Ask for a copy or a correction of your account information by emailing us.
  • Delete saved contacts in the app, or delete your whole account (see below).
  • Turn off notifications in your phone's settings.
  • Withdraw consent by deleting your account. This does not affect processing already done.
  • Raise a concern with us at wecanserve23@gmail.com. We will reply as soon as we can.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app (Profile → tap your profile card → Delete Account) or request deletion without the app on our account deletion page. This permanently deletes your account, saved contacts, folders, notifications, profile photo and purchase records, except payment records we are legally required to keep.

Children

ScanMyCard is a business tool and is not directed at anyone under 13. You must be 18 or older (or have a parent's or guardian's permission) to make purchases. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

International processing

Our providers (Google, Cloudflare, Expo) run global infrastructure, so your data may be processed on servers outside India. They are bound by contracts and security standards to protect it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or by email.
