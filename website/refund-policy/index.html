Refund & Cancellation Policy

ScanMyCard sells prepaid digital credits. Here is exactly when you can get your money back.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

What you are buying

ScanMyCard, a service of WeCanServe, sells prepaid digital scan credits for the ScanMyCard app. 1 credit lets you scan 1 business card. We do not sell physical goods, and nothing is shipped.

Delivery

Delivery: credits are delivered digitally to your account immediately after successful payment; no physical shipping. You can see your updated credit balance in the app.

Credits never expire

Credits stay in your account until you use them. There is no time limit.

Refunds

Because credits are delivered instantly and can be used straight away, purchases are non-refundable once the credits have been added to your account, except in these cases:

  1. You were charged but the credits were not added. In most cases they are added automatically within a few minutes. If they are not, contact us within 7 days with your Razorpay payment ID, and we will either add the credits or refund you in full.
  2. You were charged more than once for the same order. Duplicate charges are refunded in full.
  3. A scan fails. Failed scans never consume credits. The credit is returned to your balance automatically, so no refund request is needed.

We will also give a refund wherever the law requires one.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are sent back to your original payment method (UPI, card, netbanking or wallet) through Razorpay, and usually reach you within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank.

Cancellation

There is no subscription to cancel and nothing renews automatically. You only pay when you choose to buy a pack. You can stop using the app at any time, and you can delete your account whenever you like. Unused credits are lost when an account is deleted and are not refunded.
