What a visiting card scanner does

A visiting card scanner turns a paper card into a digital contact. Instead of typing each name and number into your phone, you take a photo and the app reads the text for you. Good scanners also understand what each line is: which one is the person's name, which is the company, which number is a mobile and which is an office line.

ScanMyCard uses Google Gemini AI for this, so it copes with busy designs, logos, two-column layouts, vertical cards and cards with details on both lines. You always see the result before it's saved, and you can correct any field.

Why people use ScanMyCard

  • 8 fields from every card: name, company, designation, phone, email, website, country and address.
  • Whole stacks, not one card at a time: scan card after card, or pick many photos from your gallery at once. The app processes them in batches.
  • Save to phone contacts: one tap adds the card to your phone's contacts. If it has two numbers, choose one or save both.
  • WhatsApp, call and email: start the follow-up straight from the contact page.
  • Folders, notes and tags: keep each exhibition, city or client in its own folder, and note where you met.
  • Duplicate check: scan a card you already have and the app offers to skip it or update the old contact.
  • Excel export: every contact, or one folder, as an .xlsx file. See business cards to Excel.

Who it's for

Anyone who comes home with a pocket full of cards: sales and business development teams, exporters and importers at trade fairs like IHGF, real-estate agents after site visits, pharma and medical reps, event organisers, founders, freelancers and small business owners. If you collect more than a handful of visiting cards a month, typing them is the slowest part of your follow-up.

Tips for a perfect scan

  • Use good light and avoid strong reflections on glossy cards.
  • Keep all four corners of the card inside the frame.
  • Choose Horizontal or Vertical to match the card.
  • Hold your phone steady for a moment while the photo is taken.
  • For cards someone sent you as photos, use Gallery instead of the camera.

If a card can't be read at all, you're not charged: the credit goes back to your account automatically.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is ScanMyCard free?

You get 10 free scans when you sign up and verify your email. After that, prepaid packs start at ₹99 + GST for 50 cards, and credits never expire.

Can it read vertical visiting cards?

Yes. Choose Vertical on the scanner for portrait cards and Horizontal for landscape ones.

Does it work with cards in my phone's gallery?

Yes. Tap Gallery on the scanner and pick many photos at once.

Can I save the scanned card to my phone contacts?

Yes. Open the contact and tap Save to phone contacts. If there are two numbers, choose one or save both.
