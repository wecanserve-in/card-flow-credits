The manual way vs ScanMyCard

Typing a card into a spreadsheet means copying around 8 fields: name, company, designation, phone, email, website, address and country. At about 2 minutes a card, 100 cards is more than 3 hours of typing, and mistakes in phone numbers and emails are easy to make.

With ScanMyCard the AI does the typing. You photograph the cards, glance over each result and fix the odd smudged digit. The same 100 cards take roughly 10 minutes. (Our estimate, not a study: your time depends on the cards and your connection.)

One sheet per event or client

Create a folder for each trade fair, city or client, for example "IHGF Delhi · Oct" or "Dealers, West zone". Export just that folder and you get a sheet with only those contacts, ready to hand to your sales team.

Sharing and importing

  • Share the file on WhatsApp, email, Google Drive or any app on your phone.
  • Open it in Google Sheets to work on it with your team.
  • Most CRMs can import an Excel or CSV file, so the sheet is a quick way to move new leads into your CRM.

Tips for a clean sheet

  • Add notes and tags while the meeting is fresh: they become the Notes and Tags columns.
  • When the app says a card is already in My Cards, choose Skip or Update to avoid duplicate rows.
  • Review each card before saving, especially phone numbers with unusual formats.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What format is the export?

A standard .xlsx Excel file with a header row and filter dropdowns. It opens in Excel, Google Sheets and WPS Office.

Can I export only some contacts?

Yes. Export all your contacts, or just one folder.

Are my notes and tags exported?

Yes. They appear in the Notes and Tags columns.

Can I get the sheet on WhatsApp?

Yes. After exporting, share the file to WhatsApp, email, Google Drive or any other app on your phone.
